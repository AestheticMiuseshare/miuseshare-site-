Creator Introduction

创作者简介

Self-taught guitarist, devoted to crafting guitar lessons built around compositional thinking. Self-taught pianist, skilled in piano tuning, with long-term piano and vocal practice. Self-taught saxophonist, able to perform various pop pieces fluently. With more than ten years of photography experience, independently developed music theory and composition teaching software.


After exploring and experimenting with many instruments, my creative path finally settled on composition. Early works were founded on contrapuntal techniques, gradually evolving into this aesthetic-focused, cross-cultural listening music series known as Miuseshare.
