My Artistic Perspective

Music · Lyrics · Imagery

Overview of Perspective

All my artistic creations are built upon the three-dimensional integrated aesthetic logic of music, lyrics and imagery. Music shapes rhythm and regional temperament, lyrics carry intellectual and humanistic core, and imagery delivers texture and emotional expression. All three align strictly with the AestheticMiuseshare architectural tone, forming a unified, stable and highly recognizable personal artistic style.

I. Music (Region · Coordination · Extended Sense)

1. Aesthetics of Voice Balance

For multi-voice performance, harmonic balance is the primary principle. Instrument timbres complement and set off one another. Instrumentation always serves the overall voice balance, avoiding jarring accents and maintaining a restrained, clean auditory texture.

2. Regional Meter Styling

Genres and meters are customized based on humanistic characteristics from regions across the world. Different nations and regions have exclusive rhythmic systems. Once the meter is defined, instrumentation, vocal delivery and texture all conform to the corresponding regional style to keep consistent regional spirit throughout the work.

3. Structural Extension Logic

Intro, interlude and outro are not isolated segments, but natural extensions of the main melody’s emotion: The intro lays the overall tonality; the interlude deepens immersion by letting feelings settle; the outro leaves lingering resonance to complete the emotional arc of the whole piece.

II. Lyrics (Thought · Psychology · Local Spirit)

1. Expressions with Thought & Empathy

Lyrics are more than emotional expression; they carry reflection. Gentle narrative reaches shared life experiences and spiritual resonance with audiences.

2. Depiction of Inner Emotion & Mental State

I capture inner situations, emotional shifts and psychological changes, using quiet, authentic text to portray humanity’s inner world.

3. Creation Rooted in Regional Humanities

Drawing materials from global travel notes, regional humanities and historical customs. Through scenery and stories of a land, I present its unique local spirit and humanistic power.

III. Imagery (Form · Connotation · Emotional Color)

1. Textural Form: Composition, Layers, Depth, Grain

Built from years of photographic aesthetic accumulation. I pursue balanced composition, prominent subjects, distinct layers, complete spatial depth and refined image texture, rejecting messy stacking.

2. Content & Connotation: Expressive, Meaningful, With Breathing Room

Beyond visual beauty, imagery carries narrative and contemplation. It retains observations of human life, traces of time and harmony with nature, leaving room for reflection.

3. Color Philosophy: Emotion-Driven, Aligned With Regional Humanities

Color is not for decorative flourish, and fully serves the work’s mood. Color schemes also consider regional and historical cultural backgrounds, unifying visual temperament with the themes of music and lyrics.
