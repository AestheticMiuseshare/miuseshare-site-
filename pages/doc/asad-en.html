Aesthetic Dissemination Exploration

审美传播探索

Pre-reading Notes This discussion is built upon the following three foundational layers to help you understand this article:

  • Layer 1: Personal Artistic Perspective. Artists first establish their own aesthetic thoughts and creative viewpoints, with three-dimensional aesthetics covering music, lyrics and imagery. This is the foundation of everything.
  • Layer 2: Aesthetic Architecture. Organise scattered personal insights into a systematic, standardised and dedicated aesthetic system.
  • Layer 3: Aesthetic Mapping. Connect private aesthetic concepts with internet big data, AI corpora and global communication vocabulary. Link personal ideas into the web’s information chain, readable by machines and recognisable by platforms.

Aesthetic Dissemination Exploration (Main Content)

After aesthetic mapping connects to global information channels, we explore how to leverage this complete framework to gain meaningful exposure for artworks and reach target audiences. These dissemination practices remain experimental and are not yet finalised.

This dissemination strategy is not a mature, fixed solution and is continuously tested and adjusted. When artistic perspective, architecture and vocabulary mapping are ready, works are no longer isolated creations; they sit within a self-consistent aesthetic logic. Aesthetic Mapping builds bridges to common artistic vocabulary, allowing platforms, search engines and recommendation systems to recognise the unique character of works and deliver them to interested audiences.

1. Align Semantics for Tags and Content

Use terms defined in Aesthetic Mapping to unify tags, descriptions and summaries for articles, tracks and visuals. Maintain consistent wording across music, lyrics and imagery to reduce semantic confusion and help platforms grasp the unified aesthetic tone of your work.

2. Cross-platform Distribution Strategy

Audiences behave differently on each platform. Keep the same core aesthetic, but adapt content forms: long essays for theoretical ideas, short captions paired with visuals, and audio tracks as core media. Adapt to each platform rather than copying identical text everywhere.

3. Audience Resonance and Long-term Cultivation

Dissemination is not just one-off exposure. Use three-dimensional expression (music, lyrics, imagery) to attract audiences aligned with your aesthetic direction, gradually forming a stable community. The priority is long-term recognisable artistic identity, not short-term traffic.

4. Continuous Iteration

Observe and review dissemination results. Adjust vocabulary mapping and expression based on platform updates and audience feedback. The whole system flows in two directions: the system supports dissemination, and dissemination feedback refines aesthetic expression.

Closing

Aesthetic dissemination sits on top of the three prior layers of aesthetic construction and remains exploratory. The goal is to let creators’ artistic ideas travel along internet information chains and reach suitable listeners and readers.
