Creator Introduction

创作者简介

Self-taught guitar, piano and saxophone. Authored a guitar textbook for polyphonic composition. Five years of piano performance practice, able to perform piano tuning. Created staff notation flashcards for teaching purposes and independently developed software for music theory and composition learning. Over a decade of photography experience. All of the above is self-learned.

After exploring various instruments, I settled on composition. My creation began mainly with counterpoint and later moved toward diverse expression. This gradually evolved into Miuseshare — my cross-cultural music series with refined aesthetic texture.

Through years of study and exploration, I now work as an independent art creator focusing on artistic connotation and cultural depth, presenting works across music, images and written reflections.
